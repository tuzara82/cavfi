      Basics

      What is CavFi?

      A DeFi hub on the DogeOS testnet: a swap with pools and farms, a meme launchpad, the hourly Arena game, .cav names, NFTs, badges and a leaderboard. Everything happens in smart contracts on-chain; the site only reads and sends transactions from your wallet.

      Is any of this real money?

      No. CavFi runs on the DogeOS testnet, so DOGE and every token here are test tokens with no value. Points and badges have no promised value either. It is a place to try things safely.

      Is it safe?

      The contracts are open source and self-reviewed (Slither, fuzz and invariant tests), but they are not externally audited. CavFi never asks for your seed phrase or private key: anyone who does is a scammer. Every action is a transaction you see and approve in your wallet.

      What is KingS?

      KingS is CavFi's own token. Farms and staking pay rewards in KingS, the Arena is played with KingS, and the bot earns small KingS tips for keeping things running.

      Trading

      How do swaps work?

      Swaps go through CavFi pools (constant-product, like Uniswap V2). The fee is 0.30% of each swap: liquidity providers get 0.25%, and up to 0.05% can go to the protocol. Set your slippage tolerance in the settings; if the price moves more than that before your transaction lands, it is cancelled instead of filled at a worse price.

      Why do I sometimes see a price warning?

      The site compares a pool's price with the other CavFi pools. If one pool is far off (usually a thin pool nobody has traded in), it warns you before you swap there.

      How do limit orders work?

      You lock the tokens you want to sell and choose the minimum you want back. As soon as the pools pay that price, the CavFi bot fills the order (for a 0.1% tip). You can cancel an open order at any time and get your tokens back.

      What does adding liquidity do?

      You deposit two tokens into a pool and get LP tokens back. You earn 0.25% of every swap through that pool, and you can stake the LP tokens on Farms for KingS. When prices move, your share shifts between the two tokens (impermanent loss).

      Launchpad

      How does a launch work?

      Anyone can create a token in one transaction. Each token has a supply of 1 billion: 800 million sell on a bonding curve (the price rises as people buy, with a 1% fee per trade) and 200 million are kept for the pool.

      What is graduation?

      When the curve sells out, the token graduates: the DOGE it raised and the remaining tokens become a CavFi pool, and the LP tokens are burned so that liquidity can never be pulled. From then on it trades on the normal swap.

      Can graduated memes join the Arena?

      Yes. Graduated tokens whose pool holds enough DOGE can be added to the Arena from the next round. Thin pools are kept out, because a pool with little DOGE in it is cheap to push around.

      How do I share a token?

      Open the token on the Launchpad and press Copy link or Post on X. The link opens that token directly.

      Arena

      How does a round work?
      • Every round, back the token you think will rise most against DOGE by staking KingS on it.
      • The first 10 minutes set each token's start price and the last 10 minutes set its end price, both as averages of on-chain samples, so a single trade can't decide the result.
      • Staking closes 10 minutes before the end. The biggest rise wins, and everyone who backed the winner splits the pot (minus a small fee shown on the Arena page).
      • If nobody backed the winner, the whole pot rolls into the next round.
      Does the bot affect the result?

      The CavFi bot records prices and settles rounds (for a 10 KingS tip). To keep prices moving it also makes 3 small random trades in the middle of each round, never in the start or end price windows. Token, direction and timing are random and it never looks at the bets.

      What is the Arena Champion badge?

      Back a winning token once and claim the soulbound Arena Champion badge from your account panel. The badge contract checks the win in the Arena contract itself.

      Names, NFTs and badges

      What is a .cav name?

      A readable name for your wallet, like borislav.cav. It costs 10 DOGE for 3 letters, 5 DOGE for 4 and 1 DOGE for 5 or more, and it is an NFT you own forever with no renewals. Your primary name shows in the wallet button and on the leaderboard.

      Can I send tokens to a .cav name?

      Yes. On Portfolio, press Send and type a name like borislav.cav instead of an address. The site looks the name up in the name contract and shows the address it belongs to before you confirm.

      What are daily quests?

      Every day (UTC) has 3 small quests, the same for everyone, shown on the home page. They count when your wallet makes the matching CavFi transaction that day. After the day ends the CavFi bot records your result on-chain: 20 leaderboard points per quest, 1 Royal Raffle ticket per quest, and all 3 quests 7 days in a row earns the soulbound Questmaster badge.

      How does the Royal Raffle work?

      Each week has a KingS pot. Tickets are free and come from daily quests. When the week ends, anyone can close the round and draw it a few blocks later: 3 winners get 50%, 30% and 20% of the pot, with more tickets meaning a better chance. The draw uses the hash of a block fixed when the round closes; on a testnet with one block producer that could in theory be influenced, so treat it as a game.

      What is auto-buy?

      On the Swap page, Auto-buy splits a purchase into equal buys over time, for example 24 buys, one every hour, so you get an average price. The whole amount waits in the CavFi auto-buy contract, the CavFi bot runs each buy when it is due and keeps 0.1% of it, and you can cancel any time to get the rest back. You can also have a buy skipped while the price is much worse than when you started.

      What are Living Cavaliers?

      Your Cavalier NFT changes on CavFi with what you do: a halo for a gm streak, golden rays for Early Pup, a trophy for Arena Champion, a star for Questmaster and your .cav name on a ribbon. It is drawn from on-chain data, so it updates by itself; wallets and explorers show the base art.

      What do GM and GN do?

      Say gm and gn on-chain once a day each (days run on UTC). Each one adds 10 leaderboard points. Say gm 7 days in a row for the soulbound Early Pup badge; missing a day restarts your streak.

      What are the CavFi Cavaliers?

      An NFT collection of crowned spaniels whose art and traits live fully on-chain. Stake a Cavalier to earn KingS; rarer crowns and accessories carry more weight, so they earn a bigger share.

      How do badges and invites work?

      Badges are soulbound collectibles for things you have done on CavFi; see them in your account panel. Share your invite link: you get 25% of your invitees' leaderboard points, and 3 invitees who trade earn you the Royal Herald badge.

      How are leaderboard points counted?

      On the Leaderboards page, from your on-chain activity over the last 7 or 30 days: swaps, launchpad activity, Arena stakes and wins, GM and GN, plus your invitees' share. The CavFi bot is left out of the board.

      Predict, duels, profiles and alerts

      How do DOGE price predictions work?

      On Predict, each market asks a yes/no question about the real DOGE/USD price, like "Will DOGE be above $0.20 on Sunday, 20:00 UTC?". Back YES or NO with KingS until the market closes. Everyone on the right side shares the whole pot in proportion to their stake, minus a 2% fee, so the pool sizes show the crowd's odds. If only one side was backed, everyone gets their KingS back. Testnet KingS, no real value.

      What is "Guess the DOGE price"?

      A free daily game on Predict → Free guess: until 18:00 UTC, each wallet can lock one guess of the DOGE price at 20:00 UTC. The answer is RedStone's DOGE/USD price signed at exactly 20:00:00 UTC: after 20:00 anyone can settle the day (the CavFi bot usually does), the contract checks RedStone's signatures itself, and the closest guess wins the day's KingS prize; on a tie the earlier guess wins. Nothing is staked, you only pay gas. Each guess is worth 20 leaderboard points and the day's winner gets 300.

      What are the hourly markets?

      Every hour the CavFi bot opens a quick "up or down" market: will DOGE be higher at the end of the hour than at its start? The line is DOGE's price at the start of the hour, bets close 10 minutes before the end, and if the price is exactly the same, NO wins. Only bets of 100 KingS or more earn leaderboard points.

      Who decides the price?

      RedStone, a decentralized oracle. Every market is decided by RedStone's DOGE/USD price, signed by its oracle nodes at exactly the resolve time. Anyone can resolve a market after that moment: the transaction carries RedStone's signed data and the CavFi contract itself checks the signatures (3 of RedStone's 5 primary signers) and that the data is from that exact second. Nobody, CavFi included, can choose the price. The CavFi bot opens the markets, picks the line of the daily and weekly ones and usually resolves them within a minute; if nobody resolves a market within a day, anyone can turn it into a full refund. Markets opened before this upgrade (marked v1) finish the old way: the bot reports Binance's price.

      What is an Arena duel?

      A one-on-one bet on the current Arena round: pick two of the round's tokens and a KingS stake, for example "RPUP beats KingS, 1,000 KingS". Whoever accepts takes the other token with the same stake. When the Arena round is settled, the token that rose more against DOGE (using the Arena's own start and end prices) wins, and its backer gets both stakes, with no fee. Duels can be accepted only while Arena staking is open. A tie, or too few price samples, refunds both. You can invite one person with a .cav name or address, or share the duel link with anyone.

      What is a profile?

      Every wallet has a public page at cavfi.com/u/name.cav (or the 0x address): its Living Cavaliers, badges, leaderboard rank, gm and quest streaks, Arena wins, duels, names and raffle tickets, all read from the chain. Share it on X or download it as an image card.

      Can the bot message me on Telegram?

      Yes. Press Telegram alerts on your profile, or send /watch yourname.cav to the CavFi bot. It messages you about Arena wins, unclaimed winnings, auto-buys, daily quest results, raffle prizes, predictions, duels, and when your gm streak is about to break. It only needs your public address, never a key. /unwatch stops it.

      Still stuck?

      Every contract is on GitHub and every transaction is on the explorer. The Portfolio page shows everything your wallet has on CavFi in one place. News and new features are posted on X @cavdefi.
