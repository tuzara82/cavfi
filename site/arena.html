My bets

Connect your wallet to see your bets.
1Start price

The first 10 minutes set each token's start price: an average of on-chain samples.

2Back a token

Stake KingS on one or more tokens. Staking closes 10 minutes before the end.

3End price

The last 10 minutes set the end price. Tokens need 3 samples in each window to win.

4Settle & claim

Biggest rise wins. If nobody backed it, the pot rolls into the next round.

Fair play: the CavFi bot makes 3 small random trades in the middle of each round (never in the start or end price windows) to keep prices moving. Token, direction and timing are random, and it never looks at the bets. It also records prices and settles rounds for the 10 KingS tip.
